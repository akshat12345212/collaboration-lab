# Pairing session notes

Pairing works best when both contributors share context continuously instead of dividing the task into isolated pieces.

## Before the session

- Agree on one concrete outcome.
- Confirm the relevant files and constraints.
- Decide who will drive first and when to rotate.

## During the session

- Explain decisions while making them.
- Keep changes small enough to review together.
- Capture open questions instead of silently assuming.
- Rotate the driver when the current slice is complete.

## After the session

- Review the complete diff together.
- Record both contributors accurately.
- Summarize the reasoning in the pull request.
- Leave the branch in a state another contributor can understand.
