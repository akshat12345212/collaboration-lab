# Pull-request review checklist

Use this checklist to keep reviews fast, useful, and proportionate to the change.

## Intent

- Does the pull request explain why the change is needed?
- Does the final diff match the stated scope?
- Are unrelated edits excluded?

## Correctness

- Does the change produce the intended behavior?
- Are important edge cases considered?
- Is the evidence appropriate for the risk?

## Maintainability

- Are names and structure easy to understand?
- Is documentation updated where future readers will need it?
- Can another contributor safely continue this work?

## Decision

Leave a clear approval or describe each required change with enough context to act on it.
